# Bike Sales Dashboard

An interactive data visualization dashboard analyzing bike purchase patterns across various demographic and geographic segments. This dashboard provides comprehensive insights into customer behavior, purchasing trends, and market segments.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The Bike Sales Dashboard presents multi-dimensional analysis of bike sales data through interactive visualizations and filtering capabilities, helping businesses understand their customer base and make data-driven decisions.

### Visualizations

#### 1. Average Income Per Purchase
- **Type**: Grouped bar chart
- **Dimensions**: Gender (Male/Female) and Purchase Status (Yes/No)
- **Insights**: 
  - Compare income levels between bike purchasers and non-purchasers
  - Analyze gender-based income differences
  - Identify income correlation with purchase decisions

#### 2. Customer Age Brackets
- **Type**: Line chart
- **Categories**: Adolescent, Middle Age, Old
- **Dimensions**: Purchase status comparison
- **Insights**:
  - Identify which age groups are most likely to purchase bikes
  - Track purchase trends across different life stages
  - Understand age-based market segmentation

#### 3. Customer Commute
- **Type**: Line chart
- **Distance Categories**: 
  - 0-1 Miles
  - 1-2 Miles
  - 2-5 Miles
  - 5-10 Miles
  - More than 10 Miles
- **Insights**:
  - Analyze how commute distance affects bike purchases
  - Identify optimal target distance ranges
  - Understand transportation needs of customers

## 🔍 Insights & Findings

### Age Demographics
- **Middle Age** customers represent the largest purchasing segment
- Both purchaser and non-purchaser trends peak in the middle age bracket
- Adolescent and older demographics show lower purchase rates

### Commute Distance
- **Short-distance commuters** (0-1 miles) show higher interest in bikes
- Purchase rates decline as commute distance increases
- Longer commutes (5-10+ miles) show lower bike adoption

3. **Sales Planning**

4. **Customer Segmentation**
